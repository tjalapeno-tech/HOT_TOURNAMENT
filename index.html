The Club

Built by the community, for the community

Since 1975, the Huntsville Oldtimers have brought adult players together for spirited, competitive hockey in Ontario's Muskoka region. We're entirely volunteer-run, and proud to make donations, support community outreach, and back local causes and businesses whenever we can.

Open to all skill levels, across four divisions, for one unforgettable weekend a year.

On Location

Huntsville, Muskoka

Picture it: crisp winter air on the ice, then warming up fireside with live music, bar service, and hot food right on site. When you head into town, Huntsville's cozy pubs, local breweries, and top-notch restaurants are ready for a hungry, thirsty hockey crowd. Cottage-country charm, snowy scenery, and small-town warmth — all part of the weekend.
