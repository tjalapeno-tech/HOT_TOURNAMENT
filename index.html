Proudly rooted in Muskoka since 1975, the Huntsville Oldtimers are more than just a hockey team — we’re a community. Built on camaraderie, sportsmanship, and love for the game, our club brings together players of all skill levels for competitive play. Whether you’re here to join, support, or stay up to date on events and tournaments, we’re glad to have you in the Oldtimers family.

Let’s keep the tradition alive — one faceoff at a time.
