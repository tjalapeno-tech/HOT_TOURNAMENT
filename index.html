The Club

Built by the community, for the community

Since 1975, the Huntsville Oldtimers have brought adult players together for spirited, competitive hockey in Ontario's Muskoka region. We're a proud non-profit, run entirely by volunteers — every dollar raised goes back into local charities and businesses.

Open to all skill levels, across four divisions, for one unforgettable weekend a year.
