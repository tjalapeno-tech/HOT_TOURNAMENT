Where to Stay

A few well-reviewed options near the rink, in order of distance from the Canada Summit Centre.

Rodeway Inn King William

~0.5 mi from the rink
23 King William St, Huntsville, ON

The closest option to the rink, and a solid budget pick. Recently updated rooms, walking distance to downtown restaurants.

705-789-9661

Comfort Inn Huntsville

~0.9 mi from the rink
86 King William St, Huntsville, ON

Right off the highway coming into town, next to shopping and several restaurants. Free breakfast included. A reliable, central choice.

705-789-1701

Holiday Inn Express & Suites Huntsville

~1.5 mi from the rink
100 Howland Dr, Huntsville, ON

Newly renovated, consistently well-reviewed. Free hot breakfast, indoor pool, and close to Walmart and several restaurants.

705-788-9500

Home2 Suites by Hilton Huntsville

~1.5 mi from the rink
159 Howland Dr, Huntsville, ON

Extended-stay style rooms with kitchenettes — a good fit for teams staying multiple nights. Clean, modern, hot breakfast included.

705-990-4661

Best Western Plus Muskoka Inn

~1.6 mi from the rink
86 Howland Dr, Huntsville, ON

The highest-rated option on this list. Indoor pool, fitness centre, and a plaza with shopping right across the street.

705-788-3345

Deerhurst Resort

~4.2 mi from the rink
1235 Deerhurst Dr, Huntsville, ON

A short drive further out, but a genuine Muskoka resort experience — lakefront, golf, spa, and multiple restaurants on-site. A good option if you're making a weekend of it.

1-800-461-4393

Distances are approximate (straight-line from the Canada Summit Centre at 20 Park Drive). We recommend booking early — tournament weekend fills up fast in Huntsville.
