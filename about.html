About the Tournament

A celebration of community, competition, and camaraderie — hosted annually in the heart of Muskoka.

Tournament Details

  • Dates: December 4–6th, 2026
  • Number of Games: 3 games guaranteed per team
  • Fees: $1,500 Team Registration Fee
  • Officials: Locally Officiated
  • Extras: Food & Drink, Skate Sharpening, Silent Auction, and Live Music on location

Our Mission

We aim to bring together adult recreational hockey players for spirited competition and community building, all while supporting local businesses and oragnaizations.

What to Expect

  • 3 guaranteed games per team
  • Friendly, competitive atmosphere
  • Food & Drink, Silent Auction, Live Music
  • Real-time standings and results

Who Can Join?

This tournament is open to Men's and Women's hockey teams of all skill levels, divided into age-based divisions to ensure fair and fun play.

Men's Divisions

  • 35+
  • 45+
  • 55+

Women's Division

  • Open
