About the Tournament

The Huntsville Oldtimers Tournament is a celebration of community, competition, and camaraderie.

Hosted annually in the heart of Muskoka, our event welcomes teams from across the region for an unforgettable weekend of hockey.

Tournament Details

  • Dates: December 5–7th
  • Number of Games: 3 guaranteed per team
  • Fees: $650 Deposit / $1300 Team Fee
  • Officials: Locally Officiated
  • Extras: Food & Drink, Skate Sharpening, Silent Auction, and Live Music on location

Our Mission

We aim to bring together adult recreational hockey players for spirited competition and community building, all while supporting local charities and businesses in Huntsville, Ontario.

What to Expect

  • 3 guaranteed games per team
  • Friendly, competitive atmosphere
  • Beer gardens, local food, and merchandise vendors
  • Real-time standings and results posted online

Who Can Join?

This tournament is open to Men's and Women's hockey teams of all skill levels, divided into age-based divisions to ensure fair and fun play.


Men's Divisions

  • 35+
  • 45+
  • 55+

Women's Division

  • Open
